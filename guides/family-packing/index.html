Free resource / Family travel

Pack for each person.
Check the shared bag once.

A family trip has two lists: what everyone needs, and what each person needs. Keep them separate so shared items are not packed twice and personal essentials are easier to spot.

Start here, then adjust for your destination, activities, weather and individual needs. No email or signup is needed.

Travellers: ____________________________________

Pack once for the group

  • □ Trip details and booking references
  • □ Chargers, cables and adapters you need
  • □ Day bag and reusable bags
  • □ Laundry bag
  • □ Shared activity or beach gear, if needed
  • □ Any shared items specific to your trip

Repeat for each traveller

Traveller: ______________________________

  • □ Outfits for each day, plus the extras you choose
  • □ Underwear, socks and sleepwear
  • □ Shoes suited to your plans
  • □ A layer for the expected weather
  • □ Toiletries and personal essentials
  • □ Comfort item, book or small activity, if wanted
  • □ Personal activity or swimming items, if needed
  • □ Individual must-haves that should not be shared

Last check before leaving

  • □ Look at one person’s remaining items at a time
  • □ Check the things used on the morning of departure
  • □ Move packed items out of the remaining-items list
  • □ Confirm who is carrying each shared item
  • □ Keep your list for the next trip

Make the next trip easier.

After you get home, note what you did not use and what was missing. Keep a clean copy for next time. In Suitcase Ledger, Plus can create a fresh copy of an existing trip so you can start packing again.
