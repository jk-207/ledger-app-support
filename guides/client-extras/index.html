Keep the stages separate.

  1. Draft: record the request and proposed details.
  2. Sent: keep the version you shared and where you sent it.
  3. Response: record what the client actually said and its source.
  4. Invoice reference: add it after you issue an invoice through your usual process.

This record does not independently verify approval, create a contract or generate an invoice. Keep original correspondence. Invoiced does not mean paid.
